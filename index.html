Privacy Policy

App: Buner Petro Management System
Developer: Jahangir Khan Bunery
Contact: jahanbunery@gmail.com
Last updated: 26 October 2026

Buner Petro is a petrol pump management app. It helps pump owners and their staff record fuel sales, stock, suppliers and payments, and view reports. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (used to create an account and log in). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Business information you enter: pump/business name, sales records, fuel prices, stock levels, tank details, supplier names and payments, sub-user names and roles, and activity (audit) logs.
  • Device and session information: a list of active devices/sessions linked to your account, so you can see and manage where you are logged in.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and does not contain advertising.

2. How we use the information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To store your business data and sync it between your devices and sub-users.
  • To show reports, backups and PDF exports that you request.
  • To show current fuel prices.

3. Where data is stored

Your data is stored on your device (local storage) and, when you are online, in Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), a service provided by Google LLC. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Firebase's own privacy information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with Firebase (Google) as our service provider, and with other users you yourself add to your pump (sub-users), according to the role you give them.

5. Backups and files

When you export a PDF report or backup, the file is saved to your device's Downloads folder. You are responsible for keeping these files safe. When you restore a backup, the app reads only the file you choose.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it. Pump owners can delete all business data from Settings → Delete all data. To delete your account and the data linked to it, email us at the address above from your registered email with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

7. Security

We use Firebase Authentication and access rules, encrypted connections, and role-based access (owner / sub-user, block and remove options). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so please use a strong password and keep your access codes private.

8. Children

This app is meant for business use by adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights

You can view and edit your business data inside the app, take a backup at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data as described above.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
